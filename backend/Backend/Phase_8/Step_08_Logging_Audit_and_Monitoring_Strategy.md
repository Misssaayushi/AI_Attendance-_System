# Step 08 - Logging, Audit, and Monitoring Strategy

## Goal
Provide complete observability for report delivery outcomes.

## Logging Scope
- Email send attempts
- Recipient counts
- Attachment details
- SMTP failures and retries
- Final delivery status

## Audit Tracking
- Delivery history table/record
- Keys: report type, date, recipients, status, attempts, error

## Monitoring Outputs
- Last N delivery attempts
- Success/failure rates
- Retry counts

## Output
Operationally useful email delivery observability model.
